# NodeScope 0.10.4 release notes

Status: iOS 0.10.4 (1), Android 0.10.4 (versionCode 24), from `feature/beacon-2.2` (fast-forwarded to
`main`). Builds on the 0.10.3 live map beta, which never went to store review, so the store notes cover
both. Estimated Coverage and Saved Plans, which had used these numbers on their own branch, move to 0.10.5.

## TestFlight — What to Test

This build adds Beacon 2.2 support and a few fixes on top of the live map beta. Please try:

• A Beacon source: open a node. Activity, signal and the weekly pattern now cover up to 30 days, and new Health and Heard In cards show recent activity and the regions that heard it.
• The region picker on a Beacon source. Beacon's regions appear as region groups.
• Live Packets on a Beacon source. Tap a packet to select it: the list holds, and you can Replay its route or open its details. Tap it again to go back to live.
• Channel messages in Beacon packets. When the server has the channel's key, the selected packet and its details show the sender and message.
• Beacon channels. An open channel should pick up new messages within a second or two.
• Opening any channel. It should open at the newest message.
• Android: after a replay, tap the route line. Route details show its stops, the observer that heard it, and the message when there is one.

## App Store — What's New

Live map update. Realistic Propagation animates a packet's paths together, like CoreScope, and Group Nearby Nodes can be turned off to see every node. Replays play all routes at once and follow packets out of your region. Pause Live holds the packet list and the map. Map Filters are regrouped, with a new Map Key.

Beacon servers: 30 days of node activity, node health and regions, region groups, route replays from Live Packets, decrypted channel messages when the server has the key, and channels that update as messages arrive.

Channels open at the newest message, and their previews follow your region.
