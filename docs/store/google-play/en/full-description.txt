NodeScope is an unofficial, read-only companion app for CoreScope and Beacon, the mesh network analyzers used by MeshTexas and other MeshCore communities. It connects directly to an analyzer's public API and live feed to show the same nodes, packet traffic, channel messages and observer data you'd see on the web dashboard, in a native Android app.

Available in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Beacon support is in beta; features and history vary by server.

WHAT NODESCOPE SHOWS YOU

Live map
Every known node, color-coded by role. Live packet traffic animates hop by hop as it crosses the mesh. Tap a route to see every hop, the observer that heard it, and its signal; tap a node for its details. Filter by region, role, recent activity or receiving observer, and center the map on yourself.

Explore
A network-at-a-glance dashboard of active nodes, online observers, packets and average signal, plus global search across nodes, observers, channels and packets. Save favorites and jump back to recently viewed items.

Node Check
Is your node getting out? See which observers hear it and what to fix, or run a live test while you send a flood advert. Requires a CoreScope server.

Live packets
Watch packets arrive in real time, grouped by transmission, with decoded payloads, raw data, every observation and every route. Replay any packet's path on the map.

Channels
Public channel traffic as a live chat feed. Monitor your own hashtag or private channels too: keys stay on your device, and messages are decrypted locally.

Observers
The devices feeding your analyzer: hardware, firmware, radio settings, battery and noise, plus charts of their packet types, traffic and signal.

Node analytics
Signal quality explained in plain terms, activity over time, a weekly heatmap, packet types, hop counts, observer coverage and the peers a node talks to.

Node Planner
Check a site before you install hardware. Link Profile shows the terrain between two points, line of sight, Fresnel clearance and estimated link margin. Estimated Coverage (beta) maps where a transmitter is likely to be heard. Keep studies in Saved Plans and share them as files with Android, iPhone and iPad. Results are estimates, not observed links.

GOOD TO KNOW

Bring your own analyzer. NodeScope shows data straight from the analyzer you pick: a community source such as MeshTexas, or any CoreScope- or Beacon-compatible host.

Works on a flaky connection. Recently loaded data stays available and is clearly marked as not live.

Private by design. No ads, no analytics. Signing in is optional and goes straight to your analyzer. Watched-node alerts are optional too: turning them on registers your device's push token, from Firebase Cloud Messaging, with the NodeScope push relay, which we operate and which stores no account details, locations or messages. Your location is used on your device to center the map and is never sent to an analyzer. Link Profile sends the two points you pick to Open-Meteo for elevation, and Estimated Coverage downloads terrain tiles from Amazon Web Services.
